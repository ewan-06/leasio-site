UK GDPR & Data Protection

Privacy Notice.

Last updated: November 2026. This Privacy Notice explains how Leasio collects, uses, encrypts, and protects your personal data under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who We Are (Data Controller)

Leasio ("we", "us", "our") is the data controller responsible for the personal data collected through leasio.co.uk.

  • Contact Email: enquiries@leasio.co.uk
  • Registered Address: 61 Bridge Street, Kington, Hereford, HR5 3DJ

2. The Personal Data We Collect

When you register an account, configure a vehicle, save a quote, or submit an enquiry, we collect:

  • Identity Data: First name, last name, and date of birth.
  • Contact Data: Email address, telephone number, and residential or business address.
  • Vehicle & Quoting Data: Selected vehicle make, model, derivative, contract duration, annual mileage allowance, initial rental multiple, and personal/business VAT preference.
  • Account Security Data: Encrypted contact fields and one-way cryptographically hashed passwords.

3. Why We Use Your Data & Lawful Bases (UK GDPR Article 6)

  • Preparing & Delivering Lease Quotes (Contract / Pre-Contractual Steps — Art. 6(1)(b)): We process your name, email address, telephone number, address, and date of birth to generate accurate lease quotations, verify eligibility criteria (such as minimum age and UK mainland delivery location), and respond to your vehicle enquiries.
  • Managing Your Customer Account (Contract — Art. 6(1)(b)): We use your email address, telephone number, and password credentials to authenticate sign-ins, store saved quotes for up to 30 days, and allow you to manage your profile details.
  • Platform Security & Fraud Prevention (Legitimate Interests — Art. 6(1)(f)): We monitor enquiry and login activity to protect our platform and users from unauthorised access or misuse.
  • Legal & Regulatory Obligations (Legal Obligation — Art. 6(1)(c)): We retain necessary records to comply with applicable UK tax, financial, and regulatory obligations.
  • Marketing Communications (Consent — Art. 6(1)(a)): We only send promotional offers if you have separately opted in, and you may withdraw consent at any time.

4. How We Encrypt & Protect Your Data

  • Field-Level Encryption: Your email address, telephone number, and residential address are encrypted before being stored in our database.
  • One-Way Password Hashing: Passwords are hashed using industry-standard cryptographic hashing (bcrypt) and are never stored in plain text.
  • Encrypted Transport: All traffic between your browser and our servers is secured via HTTPS (TLS).

5. Sharing Your Information

We never sell your personal data. We only share data where necessary to fulfil your quote or operate our platform:

  • Authorised Leasing Funders, Brokers & Dealerships: When you ask us to progress a vehicle quote or enquiry, we may share relevant details with our authorised finance partners or supplying dealers to confirm stock and underwriting terms. We will always inform you before any formal finance application or credit check takes place.
  • Trusted Infrastructure Providers (Data Processors): Cloud hosting, database, and transactional email providers acting strictly under data processing terms.

6. Data Retention

  • Saved Quotes: Automatically deleted after 30 days.
  • Customer Accounts: Retained while your account remains active, or deleted sooner upon your request.
  • Enquiry Records: Retained for up to 24 months following an enquiry (or longer where required for an active lease agreement or statutory compliance).

7. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

Under the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete personal details (including editing your email, phone number, address, and password directly in your Account Dashboard).
  • Erase your personal data ("right to be forgotten") where no overriding legal obligation requires retention.
  • Restrict or Object to specific processing activities, including direct marketing.
  • Data Portability to request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, email enquiries@leasio.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
