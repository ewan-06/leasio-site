Personal Contract Hire (PCH) Explained

Everything you need to know about leasing a brand-new car for personal use with fixed monthly payments and no depreciation worries.

How does Personal Contract Hire work?

Personal Contract Hire is a long-term rental agreement typically lasting between 24 and 60 months. You pay an initial rental upfront followed by fixed monthly payments (including VAT), and simply hand the vehicle back at the end of the term.

Key Benefits

  • Fixed monthly costs: Budget with confidence knowing your monthly rental won't change.
  • Road tax included: Vehicle Excise Duty is covered for the entire length of your contract.
  • Manufacturer warranty: Drive a brand-new vehicle covered under full warranty.
  • No depreciation risk: Hand the keys back at the end without worrying about resale value.
