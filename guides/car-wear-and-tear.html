Car Fair Wear & Tear Guide

A clear, everyday summary of the BVRLA car wear and tear standard so you know what counts as normal driving wear and what can lead to end-of-lease charges.

What “Fair Wear and Tear” Means

Fair wear and tear is the gradual, everyday ageing a car picks up through careful, normal driving. It does not cover accidents, bumps, neglect, missing items, or unapproved changes to the vehicle. If an issue goes beyond normal wear, you may be charged for it when the car goes back unless you get it repaired properly beforehand.

Golden Rule

The vehicle should be safe, roadworthy, clean, and complete with all keys, documents, and standard equipment when returned.

What Is Usually Acceptable vs. Chargeable

Area Usually Acceptable (No Charge) Likely Chargeable
Scratches & scuffs Light surface scratches up to 25mm (max 4 per panel) that do not expose primer or bare metal. Scratches over 25mm, more than 4 on one panel, or any mark down to primer, bare metal, or rust.
Dents Small, shallow dents under 15mm (max 2 per panel) where the paint is unbroken. Dents 15mm or larger, 3 or more per panel, broken paint, or any dent on the roof or a body crease/swage line.
Stone chips Minor stone chips under 3mm (within normal panel limits) with no rust. Chips 3mm or larger, excessive chip clusters, or any chip that has started to rust.
Alloy wheels & tyres Light alloy scuffs up to 50mm around the rim edge; tyres with legal tread (at least 1.6mm) and even wear. Alloy scuffs over 50mm, cracks or dents in wheels, bald/illegal tyres, sidewall cuts/bulges, or mismatched/wrong-spec tyres.
Glass, lights & mirrors Light glass marks outside the driver’s main view that pass an MOT; intact lights and mirrors. Windscreen cracks or chips in the driver’s view or failing an MOT, cracked/broken light lenses, or damaged/loose mirrors.
Interior & equipment Light creasing or normal wear on seats, carpets, and switches that cleans up easily. Burns, rips, tears, deep stains, lingering smoke/pet odours, missing keys, missing manuals/service history, or missing parcel shelf/charging cables.

Simple End-of-Lease Checklist

Check your car around 8 to 10 weeks before return so you have time to sort any issues on your own terms:

  • Wash and dry the car in good daylight so hidden scuffs, dents, and chips are easy to spot.
  • Check tyres and wheels for legal tread depth, even wear, and rim scuffs.
  • Inspect the cabin for stains, odours, tears, or warning lights on the dashboard.
  • Gather everything that came with the car, including both keys, locking wheel nut key, service records, handbooks, parcel shelf, and EV/hybrid charging cables.
  • Remove personal data (sat-nav history, paired phones, and garage/toll tags) and take clear photos before collection.
