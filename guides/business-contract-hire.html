Business Contract Hire (BCH) Made Simple

A practical guide for limited companies, sole traders, and partnerships looking to run modern vehicles without tying up company cash.

What is Business Contract Hire?

Business Contract Hire lets your business lease vehicles over a set period—typically 24 to 60 months—with an agreed annual mileage limit. You pay an initial rental followed by fixed monthly payments, then hand the vehicle back at the end of the agreement.

Why businesses choose BCH

  • Predictable cash flow: Fixed monthly rentals make budgeting easier for company accounts.
  • VAT benefits: VAT-registered businesses can typically reclaim 50% of the VAT on lease rentals for cars used partly personally (or up to 100% if used strictly for business with no personal use), plus 100% on maintenance elements.
  • Corporation tax relief: Lease rentals can generally be offset against taxable profits (subject to CO2 emission rules—always confirm specifics with your accountant).
  • No resale hassle: You don't have to worry about selling used vehicles or taking a hit on depreciation.

Choosing the right setup for your business

Start by estimating realistic annual mileage so you aren't caught out by excess mileage charges later, and pick a contract length that fits how often you like to refresh your fleet. If your employees drive company cars personally, keep Benefit-in-Kind (BiK) tax in mind—electric and low-emission vehicles are often much cheaper on BiK.
